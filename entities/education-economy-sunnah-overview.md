# Education and Economy in the Sunnah

**Arabic:** التربية والاقتصاد في السنة

## Overview

This two-part work by Dr. Yusuf al-Qaradawi examines how the Prophetic Sunnah established foundational principles in two interconnected domains: education and economics. Chapter 1, "The Sunnah and Education," draws on Sound (Sahih) and Good (Hasan) hadiths to demonstrate that the Prophet Muhammad — though unlettered — laid down comprehensive educational values that many now misattribute to modern Western thought. Al-Qaradawi enumerates nineteen prophetic teaching principles, including learning etiquette, sincerity of intention, gradual instruction, cheerfulness toward students, mercy toward learners, rewarding excellence, and employing similitudes and stories as pedagogical tools. A central theme is the Prophet's individualized approach: he gave different advice to different questioners, different answers to the same question depending on the questioner's condition, varied his manner toward Bedouins versus close Companions, tailored obligations to each person's ability, and accepted from some what he rejected from others — all illustrating the cardinal principle of accounting for individual, environmental, and temporal variations in education. Chapter 2 turns to economic ethics in the Sunnah, completing the argument that prophetic guidance anticipated and surpasses both modern pedagogical and economic thought.
