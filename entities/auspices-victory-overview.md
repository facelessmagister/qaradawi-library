## Overview

_Auspices of the Ultimate Victory of Islam_ (بشارات النصر المبين) is an eschatological and strategic work by Dr. Yusuf al-Qaradawi that counters the despair and frustration afflicting the Muslim ummah by marshalling evidence that Islam's ultimate triumph is divinely assured. Qaradawi argues that many preachers have misinterpreted End-of-Time hadiths to suggest that disbelief will only grow stronger and goodness will only decline—a reading he calls a "grave mistake" arising from selective citation of partial texts while ignoring conclusive signs (bushrá, auspices) that the future belongs to Islam.

The book identifies four urgent reasons for its subject: (1) the Prophetic injunction to give glad tidings, not cause aversion; (2) the corrosive psychological warfare waged by Islam's enemies—both external powers and internal rulers—to extinguish the Islamic awakening; (3) the need to arm Muslims with a counter-narrative of hope against campaigns that brand Islamic revival as extremism, terrorism, or fundamentalism; and (4) the widespread misconception among even pious believers that the ummah's future is nearly pitch-dark.

Qaradawi grounds his argument across five complementary sources of auspice: the Noble Qur'an, the sanctified Sunnah, the lessons of Islamic history, the realities of the present age, and the immutable Laws of Allah in creation. Each of these, he contends, confirms the Promise of Allah that His final Message will prevail. The work thus serves as both a corrective to fatalistic eschatology and a motivational manual for preachers and activists to persist in da'wah with certainty and optimism.
