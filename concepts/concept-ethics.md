# Ethics / Akhlaq (أَخْلَاق)

> **This is a redirect page.** The canonical concept page for Ethics/Akhlaq (أَخْلَاق) is **[[concept-character|Character / Akhlaq (أَخْلَاق)]]**.

The Arabic term أَخْلَاق (*akhlāq*) — commonly translated as "ethics" or "moral character" — is the preferred term in Qaradawi's corpus. The English word "ethics" maps directly to *akhlāq*, which is identical to the term translated as "character" in this wiki. Since *akhlāq* encompasses both moral character and ethical theory, and since the comprehensive page already exists under **concept-character**, this page serves as a redirect alias.

All content, citations, and discussion have been consolidated into [[concept-character]].
