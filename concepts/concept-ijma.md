# Ijma / Consensus (إِجْمَاع)

> This concept has been merged into [[concept-consensus|Consensus (إِجْمَاع)]].

Both "Ijma" and "Consensus" refer to the same Islamic legal concept (إِجْمَاع) — the unanimous agreement of qualified Muslim scholars on a legal ruling.

→ **See: [[concept-consensus|Consensus (إِجْمَاع)]]**
