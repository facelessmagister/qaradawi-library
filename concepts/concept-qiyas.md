# Qiyas / Analogical Reasoning (قِيَاس)

## Definition

**Qiyas** (قِيَاس) — "measurement, analogy" — is the fourth source of Islamic law after the Qur'an, Sunnah, and consensus (*ijma'*). It is the process of deriving a legal ruling for a new case (*far'*) by linking it to an established case (*asl*) through a shared effective cause (*'illah*). Qaradawi treats qiyas as the primary tool of ijtihad, essential for extending Islamic law to circumstances not explicitly addressed in the primary texts. He quotes Ibn al-Qayyim's definition: "Correct analogy is the Balance that God sent along with His Book." Throughout *Fiqh al-Zakah*, qiyas is the method by which zakat rulings are extended from classical asset categories (camels, dates, dirhams) to modern ones (salaries, stocks, rental income). ^[raw/extracted/fiqh-al-zakah/ch-01.txt]

## Key Positions

1. **Qiyas is indispensable for modern fiqh.** Qaradawi argues that the rates and categories of zakat "wherever there is no text, are subject to ijtihad" — and the primary form of that ijtihad is qiyas. Zakat on gold is derived by qiyas from silver; zakat on earned income by qiyas from trade goods; zakat on rental income by qiyas from agricultural produce. ^[raw/extracted/fiqh-al-zakah/ch-01.txt]

2. **Only correct analogy is valid.** Following Ibn Taymiyyah, Qaradawi holds that "analogy is an inclusive word. It includes correct analogy and incorrect analogy, but only the correct analogy is accepted in Shari'ah." Qiyas based on a genuine *'illah* (effective cause) is valid; superficial similarity without a rational connecting principle is rejected. ^[raw/extracted/fiqh-al-zakah/ch-01.txt]

3. **Zakat rates (in contrast to categories) are not derivable by qiyas.** Where the Prophet fixed specific rates — 2.5% on monetary wealth, 10% on rain-fed crops — these cannot be altered by analogy. "Jurists accustomed to applying analogy, such as the Hanafites, argue that rates of zakah cannot be derived by analogy, because their determination comes only from the text." ^[raw/extracted/fiqh-al-zakah/ch-03.txt]
