# Piety / Birr (بِرّ)

> **This is a redirect page.** The canonical concept page for God-consciousness/Piety is **[[concept-taqwa|Taqwa / God-consciousness (تقوى)]]**.

The English term "Piety" and the Arabic term بِرّ (*birr*) overlap heavily with تَقْوَى (*taqwa*) in Qaradawi's usage. Both refer to the comprehensive Islamic virtue of God-consciousness that encompasses fear of Allah, righteousness, and moral uprightness. Quranic verse 2:177 defines *birr* explicitly in terms identical to the qualities of *taqwa*.

All content, citations, and discussion have been consolidated into [[concept-taqwa]].
