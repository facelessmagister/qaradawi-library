# Tazkiyah / Spiritual Purification (تَزْكِيَة)

## Definition

**Tazkiyah** (تَزْكِيَة) — "purification, growth, sanctification" — denotes the spiritual discipline of cleansing the soul from vice and cultivating virtue, drawn from the Qur'anic verse: "Successful is the one who purifies it, and failed is the one who instills it [with corruption]" (Q 91:9-10). The concept is dual: *tazkiyat al-nafs* (purification of the soul from blameworthy traits) and *tazkiyat al-mal* (purification of wealth through zakat), both derived from the same root *z-k-w*. Qaradawi treats the Sunnah as "the agreed-upon source for the purification of the soul" and the Qur'anic verse "Take from their wealth a sadaqah that purifies and sanctifies them" (Q 9:103) as the foundational text linking financial and spiritual purification. ^[raw/extracted/approaching-the-sunnah/ch-02.txt] ^[raw/extracted/fiqh-al-zakah/ch-01.txt]

## Key Positions

1. **The Sunnah is the primary guide for tazkiyah.** Qaradawi asserts that the Prophetic pattern is "the agreed-upon source for the purification of the soul" — meaning that spiritual purification must follow the authentic Sunnah, not the invented practices of later Sufi orders that lack textual basis. ^[raw/extracted/approaching-the-sunnah/ch-02.txt]

2. **Zakat is tazkiyah of wealth; moral discipline is tazkiyah of the soul.** The two are structurally parallel: just as zakat purifies wealth by removing the portion due to Allah and to the poor, moral discipline purifies the soul by removing blameworthy traits (arrogance, envy, greed) and cultivating praiseworthy ones (patience, gratitude, generosity). ^[raw/extracted/fiqh-al-zakah/ch-01.txt]

3. **Islamic ethics grounds tazkiyah in knowledge and willpower.** In *Ethics in Islam*, Qaradawi follows the principle that "moral life is founded on knowledge and willpower." Tazkiyah is not passive mysticism but an active, knowledge-driven process of choosing virtue over vice, aided by divine guidance and sustained by worship. ^[raw/extracted/ethics-in-islam/section-01-02.txt]
