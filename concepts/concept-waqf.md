# Waqf / Islamic Endowment (وَقْف)

## Definition

**Waqf** (وَقْف) — literally "stopping, holding, immobilizing" — is the Islamic institution of endowing property in perpetuity for a charitable or public purpose. The endowed asset (land, building, revenue-generating property) is frozen from sale or transfer, and its income is dedicated to a specified beneficiary — typically mosques, schools, hospitals, or the poor. In Qaradawi's *Fiqh al-Zakah*, waqf appears primarily in the context of zakat eligibility: publicly owned assets and public trusts (including waqf properties designated for mosques, schools, and the poor) are not zakatable, while private trusts that benefit identifiable individuals (e.g., children's trusts) are zakatable because exclusive benefit accrues to specific persons. The distinction between public and private waqf has significant implications for Islamic wealth management and social welfare. ^[raw/extracted/fiqh-al-zakah/ch-01.txt]

## Key Positions

1. **Public waqf properties are not zakatable.** Qaradawi holds that assets held in public trust — mosques, schools, hospitals, and the poor as beneficiaries — are exempt from zakat because no individual holds exclusive ownership. Zakat is an obligation on individual wealth, not on communal assets held for the public good. ^[raw/extracted/fiqh-al-zakah/ch-01.txt]

2. **Private waqf and individual trusts are zakatable.** Where a waqf or trust benefits identifiable individuals (such as a family endowment for one's descendants), the zakat obligation falls on the beneficiaries because exclusive benefit accrues to specific persons who own the income. ^[raw/extracted/fiqh-al-zakah/ch-01.txt]

3. **Waqf and zakat are complementary social welfare mechanisms.** In *Economic Security in Islam*, Qaradawi treats waqf as one of the institutions — alongside zakat and sadaqah — that constitute the Islamic social safety net, ensuring that education, health, and religious infrastructure are funded independently of state taxation. ^[raw/extracted/economic-security/ch-04.txt]
