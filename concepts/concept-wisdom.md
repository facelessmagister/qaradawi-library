# Wisdom / Hikmah (حِكْمَة)

## Definition

**Hikmah** (حِكْمَة) — "wisdom, sound judgment, philosophic understanding" — is a multifaceted Qur'anic term that appears alongside the Book in the revelation: "And Allah has sent down to you the Book and the Wisdom" (Q 2:231). In the usul al-fiqh tradition, *hikmah* refers to the Sunnah as the second primary source — "the Wisdom" that accompanies and explains the Book. Qaradawi follows this classical interpretation: the Sunnah functions as the "Book and the Wisdom" paired in the Qur'anic text, meaning that God revealed not only the text of the Qur'an but also the practical wisdom of its application through the Prophet's example. In the broader ethical sense, *hikmah* denotes the intellectual and moral discernment that the believer must exercise in dawah, ijtihad, and daily life — "Invite to the way of your Lord with wisdom and good instruction" (Q 16:125). ^[raw/extracted/approaching-the-sunnah/ch-03.txt]

## Key Positions

1. **Hikmah in the Qur'an refers to the Sunnah.** Qaradawi endorses the classical position that the "Wisdom" mentioned alongside the Book in verses such as Q 2:231 and Q 3:164 is the Prophetic Sunnah. This is critical for establishing the Sunnah's authority as a parallel divine revelation rather than merely a human commentary on the Qur'an. ^[raw/extracted/approaching-the-sunnah/ch-03.txt]

2. **Wisdom is the tool of dawah and ijtihad.** The Qur'anic command to "invite with wisdom" (Q 16:125) sets the standard for how the message of Islam must be conveyed — not with harshness or oversimplification, but with discernment appropriate to the audience and circumstance. Similarly, the jurist exercising ijtihad must embody hikmah: knowledge of the sources, understanding of context, and sound judgment.

3. **Allah's creation has wisdom, even when humans cannot perceive it.** Qaradawi echoes the Qur'anic principle that "Allah created has a wisdom for its existence" (cf. Q 38:27). In *Fiqh al-Zakah*, he applies this to zakat's rates and categories: even where the effective cause (*'illah*) of a ruling is not immediately apparent, the believer trusts in its divine wisdom. ^[raw/extracted/fiqh-al-zakah/ch-01.txt]
