# Tawhid / Monotheism (تَوْحِيد)

## Definition

**Tawhid** (تَوْحِيد) — "affirming the oneness [of God]" — is the foundational doctrine of Islam, declaring that Allah is one in His essence, attributes, names, and worship. Qaradawi states in *The Lawful and the Prohibited*: "Faith (iman) is the foundation of the Islamic society, and tawhid (the belief in the Oneness of Allah) is the essence of this faith and the very core of Islam. The safeguarding of this iman and of this pure tawhid is the primary objective of all the Islamic teachings and legislations." Tawhid is not merely a theological proposition but the organizing principle of all Islamic life: it demands that worship, law, ethics, and social organization be directed solely to the One God. In *Approaching the Sunnah*, Qaradawi links tawhid to the believer's testimony on the tongue and sincerity from the heart, and in *Fiqh al-Zakah*, he notes that zakat is paired with tawhid as a socio-financial expression of the creed. ^[raw/extracted/halal-haram/ch-04.txt]

## Key Positions

1. **Tawhid is the core of Islam and the primary objective of all legislation.** All Islamic rulings — from the halal/haram binary to zakat obligations — serve to safeguard tawhid against shirk (associationism). Qaradawi writes that "the safeguarding of this pure tawhid is the primary objective of all the Islamic teachings." ^[raw/extracted/halal-haram/ch-04.txt]

2. **Tawhid and zakat are inseparable pillars.** In *Fiqh al-Zakah*, Qaradawi notes that zakat is "the socio-financial one" alongside tawhid, and that "performing zakah along with tawhid" is what distinguishes the believing community from those who reject the faith. ^[raw/extracted/fiqh-al-zakah/ch-01.txt]

3. **Shirk in any form negates tawhid.** Qaradawi treats the "war against all belief-systems which originate in man's ignorance of the divine guidance" as an imperative of tawhid. The rejection of superstition, soothsaying, and idolatrous practices is the external correlate of the internal testimony of divine unity. ^[raw/extracted/halal-haram/ch-04.txt]
