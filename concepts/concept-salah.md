# Salah / Prayer (صَلَاة)

> This concept has been merged into [[concept-prayer|Prayer / Salah (صَلَاة)]].

Both "Salah" and "Prayer" refer to the same Islamic concept (صَلَاة) — the obligatory ritual worship performed five times daily.
