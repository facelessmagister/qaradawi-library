# Mal / Wealth and Property (مَال)

## Definition

**Mal** (مَال) — "wealth, property, asset, possession" — is the central juridical concept determining what is subject to zakat and other financial obligations in Islamic law. The Qur'an uses the plural *amwal* in numerous verses: "Out of their wealth take sadaqah" (Q 9:103) and "In their wealths and properties is the right of the poor" (Q 51:19). Qaradawi provides his most detailed treatment of *mal* in *Fiqh al-Zakah*, where he endorses the Hanafi definition: *mal* is everything material that a person acquires and usually uses. This definition requires two conditions — the possibility of acquisition (*imkan al-tamlik*) and the possibility of general use (*imkan al-intifa' al-mutlaq*). Services, utilities, and non-material rights are excluded: zakat "can only be fulfilled by giving the recipient a material thing that has value." ^[raw/extracted/fiqh-al-zakah/ch-01.txt]

## Key Positions

1. **Mal must be material and acquirable.** Following the Hanafi school, Qaradawi holds that zakat applies only to material assets. Sunlight, heat, and other naturally available but unacquirable things are not *mal*; likewise, services (inhabiting a house, riding a car) and rights (custodianship) are not *mal* because they are not acquirable material objects. ^[raw/extracted/fiqh-al-zakah/ch-01.txt]

2. **The definition of mal determines the scope of zakatability.** The Malikis, Shafi'is, and Hanbalis broaden *mal* to include services and utilities. Qaradawi deliberately sides with the Hanafi position because only material assets can be collected and distributed to the eight categories of zakat recipients — a utility or a right cannot be transferred in the way the Qur'an envisions. ^[raw/extracted/fiqh-al-zakah/ch-01.txt]

3. **Unlawful wealth is not zakatable mal.** Assets acquired through theft, bribery, usury (*riba*), or oppression lack legitimate ownership and therefore fall outside the definition of *mal* for zakat purposes. "God does not accept sadaqah from stolen property." ^[raw/extracted/fiqh-al-zakah/ch-01.txt]
