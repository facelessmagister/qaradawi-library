# Nisab / Zakat Minimum Threshold (نِصَاب)

## Definition

**Nisab** (نِصَاب) — literally "origin, basis, minimum amount" — is the minimum quantity of wealth below which zakat is not obligatory. Each category of zakatable asset has its own nisab, determined by the Prophet (PBUH): 20 dinars of gold (approximately 85 grams), 200 dirhams of silver (approximately 595 grams), 5 wasaq of crops (approximately 653 kg), and the various livestock thresholds. Nisab ensures that zakat falls only on those with genuine surplus wealth, not on those barely meeting their basic needs. Qaradawi discusses nisab in detail in *Fiqh al-Zakah* and *Approaching the Sunnah*, where he addresses the modern problem of two different nisabs for gold and silver and argues for unifying the monetary nisab based on gold. ^[raw/extracted/fiqh-al-zakah/ch-03.txt] ^[raw/extracted/approaching-the-sunnah/ch-03.txt]

## Key Positions

1. **Nisab is prophetic in origin, not subject to analogical derivation.** The specific nisab amounts for gold, silver, crops, and livestock were set by the Prophet and cannot be changed through qiyas. Qaradawi endorses the principle that "rates, wherever there is no text, are subject to ijtihad" — but the nisab thresholds themselves are textually fixed. ^[raw/extracted/fiqh-al-zakah/ch-03.txt]

2. **The nisab for monetary wealth should be unified on gold.** The Prophet decreed two separate nisabs because gold and silver were the two currencies of his time. Today, with paper currency backed by neither, Qaradawi argues for "the definition of a single nisab in our age for money" based on the gold nisab, since the silver nisab has become dramatically undervalued relative to the gold nisab. ^[raw/extracted/approaching-the-sunnah/ch-03.txt]

3. **Nisab protects the poor from zakat.** The nisab threshold excludes those who barely have enough to meet their needs, ensuring that zakat is an obligation of surplus, not of subsistence. This aligns with the broader Qur'anic principle that Allah does not burden a soul beyond its capacity.
